About JMJ Enterprises Solutions

Who We Are

Your Safety & Cleanliness, Our Business.

JMJ Enterprises Solutions is a Delhi-based security and facility management company providing trained security personnel and professional cleaning staff to corporate offices, industrial sites, hospitals, educational institutions, hotels and residential societies across the region.

Every guard and housekeeping staff member we deploy is trained, background-verified and supervised by our in-house operations team, so you get consistent, accountable service every single day.

  • Trained & background-verified staff
  • 24/7 operations & supervision
  • Serving corporate, industrial & residential clients
  • Flexible contracts tailored to your site
