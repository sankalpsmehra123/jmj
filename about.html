Who We Are

A Trusted Single-Source Partner

JMJ Enterprises Solutions Ltd is an ISO 9001:2015 and PSARA-2005 certified provider of security guarding and facility management services - a trusted partner to banks, embassies, government institutions and corporates across India.

  • Founded in 2013 by Mr. Kumar Sanu, who belongs to a bureaucrat family and having a legal background, scaled steadily over a decade
  • Fully vetted, well-trained manpower, licensed under the PSARA Act
  • Tie-ups with reputed training centres affiliated to the respective authorities
  • Growth led by client recommendations and high contract retention
  • Offices in Delhi (HQ), Noida, Gurgaon and Bengaluru
